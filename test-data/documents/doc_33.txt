Step 1: Launch iTunes on your computer, and then connect iPod/iPad/iPhone to PC. Step 2: Click the name of your iPhone/iPod/iPod under Devices in the left column. Step 3: Choose Books above the main iTunes window. Step 4: Select audiobooks you want to transfer.*Click Sync Audiobooks and then choose the audiobooks you want to transfer or select All Audiobooks to transfer all of them to the iPhone/iPod/iPad. Click Apply at the bottom of the screen, you can finish syncing process.) Click Import, you can add new audiobooks from PC to your iOS devices. Syncios Audiobook Transfer is a two ways transfer for all files, so you can not only backup files to PC, transfer audiobooks from PC to your iPod/iPhone/iPad is also available.
