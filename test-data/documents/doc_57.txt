There are two main problems when it comes to hard-boiled eggs: green-gray yolks and shells that just won’t peel off. Make hard-boiled eggs headache-free by following this easy method. First, place the cold eggs in a single layer in a pan with enough cold water to cover them by about 1 inch. Then, place the pan on the stove over high heat. Shutterstock. As soon as the water comes to a boil turn the heat off, cover the pan, and remove it from the heat. Allow the eggs to sit in the water until they are cooked; about 9 minutes for a medium egg, 12 minutes for a large egg, and 15 minutes for an extra-large egg.
